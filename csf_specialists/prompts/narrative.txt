Do NOT write anything outside the JSON. Do NOT include the JSON schema.

===================== HOW TO WRITE =====================

Register: the tone of a partner-authored advisory report. Direct, calm, specific.

- Plain English. No security jargon. If you must name a control, say what it does.
  Write "requiring a second form of sign-in", not "MFA enforcement posture".
- Say what is strong as readily as what is weak. A report that only lists problems
  reads as an audit, not an assessment. Where a Function scores well, say so plainly.
- Be specific to THIS organisation. "Access controls on newer platforms are strong;
  equivalent rigour on legacy infrastructure is the leading opportunity" is useful.
  "Access control maturity varies" is not.
- No hedging ("may potentially", "it appears that"), no filler ("it is important to
  note"), no exhortation ("the organisation must urgently").
- Never invent a finding, a system name, a date, a number, or a regulator. If the
  evidence below does not support a statement, do not make it.
- British English spelling (organisation, prioritise, recognised).

===================== WHAT TO PRODUCE =====================

1. `intro` — one paragraph, 4-6 sentences, opening the executive summary. Describe how
   this organisation manages cybersecurity overall, then where its strongest practice
   and its best opportunities for improvement currently lie. Name the Functions.
   Do not list every score; this is prose, not a table.

2. `function_summaries` — one entry per Function listed in RESULTS BY FUNCTION above,
   in the order given. Each `summary` is 1-2 sentences describing what that Function's
   practice actually looks like at this organisation: its strongest aspect and its
   leading opportunity, in that order where both apply. These sit in a six-card grid
   and are read side by side, so keep them close to the same length and never start
   two of them with the same words.

3. `improvement_themes` — 4 to 6 themes, most material first. A theme is a pattern
   across several Subcategories, not a restatement of one. Group the weakest
   Subcategories into the smallest number of real themes that covers them.
   - `title`: a short noun phrase, no verb, no colon.
   - `body`: 2-3 sentences — what is missing today, and what it costs the business to
     leave it. Describe the direction of travel, not a project plan.
   - `refs`: the Subcategory ids from the evidence above that this theme covers.
     Only ids that actually appear above.
